NextCuatro — Features & Instructions

Flexible. Smart. Adaptive.

NextCuatro simplifies organizing pickleball, from casual drop-in sessions to skill-based rotations and structured tournaments.

Choose from three play modes: Open, Zoned, or Tournament.

1. Open Play

Open Play is designed for flexible, drop-in pickleball sessions where players may arrive, leave, or take breaks throughout play. NextCuatro automatically generates court assignments while promoting fair playing time and varied matchups.

Three Rotation Options

Smart Random

Creates varied matchups while considering playing time, sit-out fairness, and partner variety. Players do not need skill ratings.

Similar Skill

Groups players according to skill level, with stronger players generally assigned to lower-numbered courts. Players typically remain within their skill group or move one court higher or lower. Fixed partnerships and attendance may occasionally require exceptions.

Balanced Teams

Creates competitive games by balancing the combined skill ratings of opposing teams.

Similar Skill and Balanced Teams require player skill ratings.

Open Play Features

  • Supports up to eight courts.
  • Add or remove players throughout a session.
  • Allow temporary breaks and returns to play.
  • Keep two players together as fixed partners (pair or unpair a team, or play solo).
  • Automatically distribute playing opportunities and sit-outs.
  • Minimize repeated partners and matchups.
  • Track games played and sit-outs.
  • Optionally track wins and losses and adjust individual skill ratings based on recorded results (adaptive ratings).
  • Use an optional 10-, 12-, or 15-minute game timer.

Select Next Cuatro! to generate the next round.

2. Zoned Play

Zoned Play organizes players into skill-based groups, allowing participants to play primarily with others of similar ability. Firm skill boundaries separate the six zones. Unlike Similar Skill in Open Play, players do not cross into other zones unless they change their rating. Zones function independently of one another.

Zoned Play Features

  • Assign available courts to individual skill zones.
  • Each zone operates independently, with its own rotations.
  • Players remain within their assigned skill zones.
  • Fairness tracking helps distribute playing time.
  • Court assignments can be adjusted as attendance changes.

Adaptive ratings and the shared game timer are disabled in Zoned Play.

3. Tournament Play — Under Development

Tournament Play is designed for organized competitions using a fixed roster and structured game schedule. Tournament Play remains under development and is not yet recommended for live tournament use.

4. Player Management

NextCuatro allows organizers to manage participants as attendance changes.

Add Player: Add participants before or during a session.

Skill Rating: Select a descriptive skill level (Beginner through Elite), enter a numerical rating (2.0–5.0+), or leave a player unrated. Descriptive levels use representative numerical values for scheduling.

Pair Players: Designate two players who prefer to play together. Partnerships can be changed during a session, or players can go solo again.

Sit Out / Bye: Allow a player to take a temporary break while remaining part of the session.

Done Playing / Leave: Remove a player from active rotation while retaining their session history.

Restore: Make a previously departed player available again.

New arrivals receive priority for upcoming rotations when court space permits, without being penalized for games played before they arrived.

5. Fairness & Court Assignments

NextCuatro uses an adaptive rotation system designed to distribute playing opportunities fairly.

When generating games, the system considers:

  • Previous sit-outs and voluntary breaks.
  • Consecutive games played.
  • Partner variety and repeated matchups.
  • Fixed partnerships.
  • Available courts and player skill levels.

The objective is to give participants reasonably equal opportunities to play while maintaining enjoyable matchups.

Fixed Partnerships

Two players can be paired so they generally play together.

Whenever possible, fixed partners also sit out together. Occasionally, keeping a pair together would create an unfair advantage in playing time. In these situations, NextCuatro may offer the organizer the option of temporarily separating the pair for two games so that each partner then takes one sit-out. The organizer can decline the separation. NextCuatro preserves accurate sit-out totals and limits repeated requests according to its fairness rules.

Physical Courts

Organizers can select which numbered courts are available, from Court 1 through Court 8. Nonconsecutive courts can be selected. Changes to court availability take effect with the next game.

In Smart Random and Balanced Teams, NextCuatro also considers court usage and repeated groups of four. Similar Skill maintains its ordered skill ladder.

6. Game Results & Adaptive Ratings

In Open Play, organizers can record game results by selecting the winning team.

NextCuatro tracks session statistics, including:

  • Games played.
  • Actual sit-outs.
  • Wins and losses (when results are recorded).
  • Partner variety.

Adaptive Skill Ratings

Players who enter a numerical skill rating may optionally enable adaptive ratings. Adaptive ratings are OFF for descriptive skill levels; selecting Adaptive prompts the organizer to choose a numerical rating instead.

When enabled, NextCuatro can gradually adjust their skill rating based on recorded wins and losses against other rated players.

Players without an initial rating cannot use adaptive adjustments.

Adaptive ratings are paused in Zoned Play.

7. Timer & Session Controls

Open Play includes an optional game timer with three settings:

10 minutes · 12 minutes · 15 minutes

The timer can be started, paused, or reset.

When time expires, NextCuatro displays an end-of-game message and sounds a notification.

Selecting Next Cuatro! generates the next round and resets the timer. The timer does not restart automatically.

Saving Your Session

Session information is stored locally in the browser on the device being used.

Closing the browser does not necessarily erase the session, but clearing browser data may remove saved information.

New Session / Clear Everything requires confirmation before erasing the current session.
